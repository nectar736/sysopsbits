Frequently asked questions

Are these real exam questions?

No. Every practice question is original, written from the public exam skills outline and official documentation. Leaked exam content breaks Microsoft's, PMI's, and Scrum.org's exam agreements, and it often teaches the wrong answers anyway.

Is the content current?

Each guide is written against the exam's published skills outline at the time of writing. Outlines change, so before you book, compare the outline on the official exam page with the guide's table of contents.

How do I get the file after paying?

Buy Me a Coffee shows a download link as soon as payment completes, and the link is also in your receipt email. If the download doesn't arrive, contact us with the email you used at checkout.

Should I start with the free practice tests?

Yes. Take the free AZ-900, AZ-104, or PMP practice test first. If you score well below the passing mark, the guide's full practice exam and domain-by-domain notes show where to focus.
