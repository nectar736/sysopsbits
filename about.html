About Me

I'm a PMP-certified Systems Administrator with deep experience in cloud infrastructure, Microsoft 365, Azure networking, Entra ID, Intune, and hybrid enterprise environments. Backed by Microsoft, CompTIA, Cisco, and project management certifications, I focus on delivering secure, scalable, and efficient solutions that align technology with business goals, improve operations, and support successful project outcomes.

My background includes Windows Server, SCCM, Meraki, PowerShell, and Microsoft Graph automation, with a strong emphasis on identity, compliance, and endpoint management.

I started SysOpsBits to publish the labs, notes, and practice questions I built while earning these certifications. Every tutorial is something I have run myself, and the Microsoft and PMP guides are written by someone who holds those certifications. When Microsoft or PMI change an exam outline, the affected guides get reviewed. If you spot something out of date, tell me.

About SysOpsBits

SysOpsBits was created with a simple goal: provide clear, actionable, and unbiased information to help sysadmins and DevOps engineers make better decisions about their infrastructure.

Here’s what we cover:

  • In-depth tutorials — Step-by-step guides you can implement immediately.
  • Honest comparisons — Real-world testing and cost breakdowns to help you choose the right tools.
  • Tool reviews — Hands-on testing of the latest DevOps and sysadmin tools.
  • Cost optimization — Find the most cost-effective solutions without sacrificing performance.

Our Values

  • Practical over theoretical: We focus on what works in real environments.
  • Honest recommendations: We only recommend what we've actually tested.
  • Transparent affiliations: We clearly disclose when content contains affiliate links.
  • Community-driven: We welcome feedback and suggestions from our readers.
